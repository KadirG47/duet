Questions

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

Duet isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen warns about new apps. Click More info → Run anyway. Official downloads are only on this site and on GitHub.

Do I need a Minecraft account?

Yes — you sign in with the Microsoft account that owns Minecraft: Java Edition, on Microsoft's own page. Duet never sees your password. Microsoft sign-in is currently waiting for Mojang's approval for new launchers.

Can I bring my stuff from another launcher?

Yes. On first start Duet asks whether to copy your instances from the Modrinth App, Dawn (Feather), Prism, MultiMC, CurseForge, ATLauncher, GDLauncher or the official launcher — worlds, mods, packs, settings and servers. It's a copy: the other launcher keeps its files, and account logins are never copied.

Who can see what I'm playing?

Only the friends you added, and only what you allow: in the Friends page you can look offline, hide what you're playing, or hide your server. Duet checks your account with Mojang the same way Minecraft servers do, so your login never leaves your PC.

A video background doesn't play on Linux

Use a .webm video, or install the codecs: sudo apt install gstreamer1.0-libav gstreamer1.0-plugins-good.
